The clinical story

A different way to handle routine line care.

SnapCath™ is built around a straightforward idea: secure the line and cover the site with one device, then access the site without peeling the adhesive base off the skin every time.

1

Secure and cover in one device.

The base, shell, and lid are designed as one system around the catheter site, reducing the number of separate products clinical teams need to apply, stock, and teach.

2

Open the lid for routine site access.

The adhesive base is designed to remain in place while the lid opens for access. That is the core workflow change behind the time target and the reduction in repeated adhesive peeling.

3

Carry the same approach across care settings.

Lenneuro is developing SnapCath™ for line care across inpatient, outpatient infusion, dialysis, long-term care, and home settings so the device and technique can stay consistent as care moves.

The device

Three parts, one line-care system.

The current SnapCath™ prototype combines an adhesive base, a rigid shell that holds the catheter, and a hinged lid that opens and closes over the site.

  • Adhesive base: attaches to the skin around the insertion site and is designed to stay in place during routine access.
  • Rigid shell: holds the catheter and sits over the site.
  • Hinged lid: opens for site access and is designed to provide coverage between checks.
  • 28-day wear objective: the supplied deck positions the device for up to 28 days of stay-in-place wear, with replaceable lids during that period.

Current design prototype. Final appearance, specifications, indications, and performance may differ.
