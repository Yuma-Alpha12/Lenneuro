Why it matters

What your letter actually does

Early-stage medical devices live or die on evidence of clinical need. A short letter from a practicing clinician is one of the most credible forms that evidence can take.

Documents clinical need

Grant reviewers and prospective partners want to hear from the people at the bedside, not from us. Your letter is direct evidence that the securement and site-coverage challenges we're designing around are ones clinicians actually encounter.

Shapes the device

What you write in your own words feeds back into design decisions. Signatories are also the first clinicians we contact when SnapCath™ reaches the point of clinical evaluation.

No commitment

No cost, no purchase obligation, no requirement to participate in any future study, and no exclusivity. Roughly three minutes of your time, and you can withdraw the letter at any point by emailing us.

In development

What SnapCath™ is designed to do

SnapCath™ is an external catheter securement accessory intended to support catheter securement and site coverage in a single device. These are its design objectives, currently being evaluated in development.

An integrated design

Designed to support catheter securement and site coverage in a single accessory.

Designed with skin in mind

A design goal of the device is to limit adhesive contact with the skin during wear.

Site access by design

Features an openable lid intended to allow routine site access without removing the adhesive.

Designed to be water resistant

Water resistance for everyday wear is a design objective and is being evaluated in development.

SnapCath™ is currently under development. It has not been cleared or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and is not available for sale or commercial distribution. Product images and models show design prototypes; final product appearance and specifications may differ. Nothing on this page is an offer to sell or a promotion of an unapproved device.
